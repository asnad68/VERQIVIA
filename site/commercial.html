WHO THIS IS FOR

A verification layer at a real business boundary.

Organizations & brands — publish a canonical identity and selected claims.

Procurement & compliance — inspect supplier identity, evidence and scope before relying on a relationship.

Marketplaces & software — resolve the same identity programmatically instead of screen-scraping.

AI agents — add a structured verification step before relying on an unfamiliar business identity.

COMMERCIAL PRINCIPLE

Buy a verification workflow, not a green badge.

The commercial value is in making a useful business claim easier to inspect and maintain. VERQIVIA does not sell a universal trust score, trademark rights or a guarantee that a company is trustworthy.
