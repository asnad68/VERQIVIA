ILLUSTRATIVE ENTERPRISE SCENARIO

How a global brand could use VERQIVIA

Example only — no partnership or endorsement is implied.

Imagine a global brand such as Apple wants external companies to distinguish official domains, authorized partners and selected corporate claims from look-alike identities.

The brand could publish one canonical VERQIVIA identity. A procurement system, marketplace or AI agent could then resolve that identity and inspect the claim-level evidence instead of assuming that the brand name alone is proof.

A visual ◇ marker can simply point to the same verification record. The marker is an access point; the evidence and lifecycle are the product.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

No universal “trust score”

VERQIVIA keeps individual claims separate. A domain can be verified while a partner relationship remains unverified. A later event can supersede an earlier event, and a revoked record remains auditable instead of disappearing.
