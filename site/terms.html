TERMS / PRODUCT LIMITATIONS

Use VERQIVIA as verification infrastructure, not as a legal authority.

These public terms describe the current prototype and early commercial boundary. Final production terms should be reviewed and adopted by the operating legal entity before commercial launch.

1 · SERVICE BOUNDARY

What VERQIVIA provides

VERQIVIA provides software and records for publishing, inspecting and exchanging organizational identities, claims, evidence references and verification events. A verification result is limited to the claim, evidence, procedure and scope stated in the record.

2 · WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVIDE

No automatic legal status.

VERQIVIA does not grant trademark rights, company registration, regulatory authorization, financial assurance, professional certification or a universal reputation score. It does not guarantee that a real-world claim remains true after a verification event is recorded.

3 · CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

Provide accurate, lawful inputs.

Participants are responsible for having the authority to submit the data and claims they ask VERQIVIA to publish or process. They should not submit private keys, passwords, seed phrases or unnecessary personal data.

4 · EVIDENCE & VERIFICATION

A reference is not automatically truth.

Evidence records identify what was considered. Verification procedures state how a claim was evaluated and within what scope. Consumers remain responsible for their own decision policies and additional checks, especially for high-impact decisions.

5 · AVAILABILITY

Prototype availability is not a production SLA.

The public prototype may change, be unavailable, or contain synthetic data. No production availability, support level or recovery objective is promised until an applicable commercial agreement and production infrastructure are in place.

BEFORE A REAL PILOT

Agree the scope in writing.

Real business data, confidentiality, fees, service levels, intellectual property and applicable law should be covered by a written agreement appropriate to the deployment.
