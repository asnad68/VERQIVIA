DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Collect less. Prove more.

A valid record is not automatically a truthful record. The prototype deliberately exposes the chain behind a result and avoids a universal trust score.

A

Companies

Publish a canonical business identity and selected claims without turning VERQIVIA into a trademark office.

B

Procurement

Inspect supplier claims, evidence and verification scope before relying on a business relationship.

C

Software

Resolve the same identity through a machine-readable profile and API instead of screen-scraping.

D

AI Agents

Give business-facing agents a structured verification step before they rely on an unfamiliar organization or domain.
