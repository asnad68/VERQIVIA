## 9. Legal boundary

VERQIVIA is not a government registration authority, trademark office, legal certification service or universal trust/reputation authority. The engagement does not grant trademark rights or guarantee that a company or claim is truthful beyond the agreed verification scope.

Final agreements should identify the operating legal entity, applicable law, dispute mechanism and any required privacy/data-processing terms.
