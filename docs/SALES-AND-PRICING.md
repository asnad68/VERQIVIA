# VERQIVIA — Sales and Commercial Packaging

## Commercial position

VERQIVIA should initially be sold as a **controlled verification infrastructure engagement**, not as an unproven mass-market trust badge.

The customer pays for a concrete business outcome:

> Make one important business identity or relationship easier to verify by people and software.

The current public site supports early commercial conversations. Production SaaS activation remains an external deployment gate.

## Initial offers

### 1. Verification Pilot

Best for the first real-world engagement.

Suggested scope:

- one organization;
- one VERQIVIA identity;
- up to five selected claims;
- agreed evidence sources;
- one or more versioned verification procedures;
- public or controlled verification view;
- portable machine-readable profile;
- API integration guidance where relevant;
- short review and findings report.

Pricing: **quote from scope** during the early-access phase.

### 2. Verification Workflow

For an organization that already knows the workflow it needs.

Possible scope:

- identity onboarding;
- domain/control verification;
- selected authorization or relationship claims;
- evidence lifecycle;
- verification-event maintenance;
- operational review.

Pricing: quote from scope.

### 3. API / Ecosystem Integration

For marketplaces, procurement systems, business software and AI-enabled workflows.

Possible deliverables:

- machine-profile integration;
- API contract integration;
- identity resolution;
- claim-level verification handling;
- revocation/supersession handling;
- integration test suite.

Pricing: quote from scope.

### 4. Enterprise Verification Layer

For larger organizations that require governed onboarding and higher-assurance operational controls.

Possible scope:

- multiple business identities;
- managed verification procedures;
- issuer/key governance;
- audit exports;
- deployment-specific controls;
- security and privacy review;
- service-level agreement.

Pricing: custom enterprise agreement after production infrastructure and legal review.

## Proposal template

Every commercial proposal should state:

1. customer problem;
2. exact verification scope;
3. identities and claims covered;
4. evidence sources and data categories;
5. deliverables;
6. acceptance criteria;
7. fee and payment terms;
8. confidentiality;
9. data retention and deletion;
10. security responsibilities;
11. intellectual-property treatment;
12. support/service level;
13. applicable law and dispute terms;
14. termination conditions.

## Pricing principles

Do not price a generic "trust score". Price measurable work and infrastructure:

- identities maintained;
- claims and verification procedures;
- verification volume;
- machine/API integration;
- managed operations;
- audit/reporting requirements;
- support and service levels.

The public project should not invent a fixed global price until pilot data shows what customers actually value and what the operating cost is.

## Public claims

Until written permission exists, do not describe a participant as a customer, partner, reseller or endorser. Do not publish logos or private verification material without authorization.

## Production gate

A commercial contract should not imply that public production is already live. Before production delivery, complete the external launch gates in docs/FINAL-REPOSITORY-READINESS.md, including authentication, persistent infrastructure, gateway/security controls, key governance, backup/restore, monitoring and jurisdiction-specific legal/privacy/tax review.
